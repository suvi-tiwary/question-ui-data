# QuickRuit Competitor & Market Analysis

## 1. Competitor overview

### Aptahire.ai
- Pricing is not fixed; plans are segmented by usage, such as around 20 interviews per month.
- Support channels include telephone and email.
- Founded in 2023.
- Headquarters: Chennai, Tamil Nadu.


### Geniehire.ai
- Offers a free starter plan.
- Includes 3 HR seats, 500 CV screenings, and 10 AI video interviews.
- Custom enterprise options are available.
- Founded in 2024.
- Headquarters: Frisco, Texas, USA; engineering and development team in India.

### Rightfit.so
- Focuses on proof-of-work hiring, evaluating what candidates have built instead of relying only on resumes or keyword matching.
- Strong positioning for employers who want skill-based validation.
- SEO blog strategy should start with a real problem, then present the solution.

### HireVue
- Founded in 2004 in the USA.
- Enterprise pricing is high and often customized, with roughly $35,000 for enterprise packages.

### Aptiview.ai / job-board
- Starter plan: 50 interviews/month for $600.
- Growth plan: 100 interviews/month for $1,000.
- Enterprise plan: customized pricing.

## 3. SEO and content opportunities

### Search intent mapping
- Transactional: Best AI recruitment software for startups
- Comparison: Rightfit vs HireVue vs QuickRuit
- Informational: How AI resume screening works
- Problem-solving: How to screen 1,000 resumes quickly
- Product research: AI resume screening software pricing
- Technical: How to build an interview question bank
- Candidate-focused: DSA interview questions by company
- Technical learning: SQL interview questions with answers
- Emerging trends: How AI agents automate recruitment
- Hiring workflow: Resume screening vs skills-based assessments

### Content strategy
- Start blog posts by introducing the real problem before presenting the solution.
- Use comparison articles to improve SEO visibility.
- Focus pages on specific hiring pain points, such as resume overload, candidate screening speed, and skill testing.

## 4. UI and landing page patterns

- Most competitor landing pages include a strong opening image, solution explanation, feature highlights, testimonials if available, pricing, and FAQs.
- Custom pricing is common across the market, especially for enterprise plans.
- Clear plan comparisons and CTA sections are important conversion drivers.

## 5. Conclusion
- Most competitors offer customized pricing for enterprise customers.
- A landing page should cover: hero section, problem/solution narrative, feature list, testimonials, pricing plans, and FAQs.
- For blog content, the best approach is to lead with the pain point and then explain the solution.
- Comparative content and product-focused SEO pages can increase organic reach and conversion potential.
